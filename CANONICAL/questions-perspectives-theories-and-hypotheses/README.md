# John’s Questions, Perspectives, Theories & Hypotheses

This collection preserves exact John-authored source objects whose primary function is inquiry, perspective-building, hypothesis formation, theory development, or explicit open questioning. It also preserves John-selected Arena.ai Deep Sessions as canonical interaction records.

## Epistemic-status disclaimer

> **These source objects and interaction records are not presented as absolute or proven.**

For John-authored sources, canonical status authenticates **authorship, wording, ordering, version, and provenance**. It does not certify every proposition as fact, establish every causal interpretation, or make a hypothesis immune from correction.

A file in this collection may intentionally contain:

- questions whose answers remain open;
- John’s personal perspectives;
- working theories or hypotheses;
- propositions that may later prove partially right, partially wrong, incomplete, or in need of refinement;
- evidence claims that require separate source evaluation;
- invitations to compare competing explanations.

Canonical status answers: **“Is this the registered source object or interaction record?”**

It does not automatically answer: **“Has every statement inside it been proven?”**

## Reading rule

Read the complete source before evaluating a fragment. Preserve its qualifiers and distinguish questions, observations, hypotheses, factual claims, examples, and conclusions rather than assigning one epistemic status to the whole file.

Working analyses, A.I. reactions, comparisons, and later corrections normally belong in the connected working or testing repositories. They must not be silently inserted into John-authored canonical source files.

## Canonical Arena.ai Deep Sessions

John-selected Arena.ai Deep Sessions may be preserved here as canonical interaction records. Their canonical status authenticates the recorded dialogue, ordering, participants, dates, and provenance. Inclusion preserves what each participant said; it does not automatically endorse every factual claim, inference, or A.I.-generated extension as a canonical conclusion of the ACCM Deep Ethics Project.

The participant layers remain distinct:

- John’s original words;
- exact A.I. responses;
- John’s corrections or extensions;
- later A.I. acknowledgements, reactions, and cross-model audits;
- externally verified facts, where separately established.

Quoted participant text remains attributed to its speaker. Spelling or layout corrections require John’s explicit approval and must not silently change meaning, qualifiers, trajectory, or provenance.

See the [Canonical Deep Sessions Index](DEEP-SESSIONS-INDEX.md).
